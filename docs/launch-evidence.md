## Materialization failure contract (AC-10)

Both `MaterializationFailure` codes (`pool_exhausted`, `missing_timing_configuration`) were forced
alongside a healthy sibling template in `tests/e2e/backend-launch.test.ts`: the healthy template
materialized to `scheduled`; both failing templates wrote nothing and were independently alerted
through a stubbed Telegram `fetch` and a fake `SendEmail` binding — no real network call was made.
`tests/scheduler-materialize-weekly.test.ts` additionally proves a still-failing occurrence alerts
again on a later hourly pass, and that neither channel nor occurrence blocks another. The weekly
retry sweep remains capped at `WEEKLY_RETRY_LOOKBACK_WEEKS = 8` (asserted directly); this is the
owner-accepted V1 limitation because the quiz season ends in the third week of November — no
backlog discovery or indefinite recovery was added.

## Remaining limitations (accepted, not defects)

- Weekly board recovery is bounded to 8 weeks; an unpublished week that ages out requires manual
  forward recomputation.
- Recurring template CRUD remains deferred (DB-seeded templates only).
- The admin report is JSON only — no CSV/PDF export.
- `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`, `TELEGRAM_ALERT_CHAT_ID`, and `EMAIL_ALERT_ADDRESS` remain blank placeholders
  in `wrangler.toml`; `ALERT_EMAIL` stays commented out. These are real account-specific values only
  an operator can supply (a Telegram group/bot, a private alert chat, and a custom Cloudflare-managed
  domain with a verified Email Routing destination) — this document does not invent them.
