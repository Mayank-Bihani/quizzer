Sprint S2 — responsive proof

Every S2 run screen at 390 (phone, student default), 820 (tablet) and 1440 (laptop). Frames are real iframes at exact CSS widths, so what you see is the breakpoint behaviour, not a scaled image. Each frame is 860px tall, so one .qcard — one run screen — fills it exactly; scroll a frame to reach the states stacked below it.

Relayout to watch for: the action foot goes from stacked (skip above, consequence line, Submit & lock below) to a single row with skip far left and Submit & lock far right at 640; the header collapses from three rows to two at 900, with identity and the window clock grouped left and progress taking the rest of the row; the stem steps up a type size at 900 and again at 1280 rather than the container being capped.

Measured, not eyeballed: every frame below reports documentElement.scrollWidth equal to its viewport at all three widths — 390/390, 820/820, 1440/1440.
