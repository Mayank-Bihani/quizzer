Every S3 run-edge screen at 390 (phone, student default), 820 (tablet) and 1440 (laptop). Frames are real iframes at exact CSS widths, so what you see is the breakpoint behaviour, not a scaled image.

Relayout to watch for: the passage set is the one screen that genuinely relayouts — a capped, independently-scrolling pane above the question on phone, a two-column split with the passage uncapped (full available height) from 900px up. The full-screen interstitials (resumed, late-join, deadline-cut) simply recentre — same `.state` / `.lockcard` content at every width, per "do not equalise".
