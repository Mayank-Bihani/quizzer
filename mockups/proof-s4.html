Sprint S4 — responsive proof

Every S4 results screen at 390 (phone, student default), 820 (tablet) and 1440 (laptop). Frames are real iframes at exact CSS widths.

Nothing here genuinely relayouts. Finish and holding are captive, centred interstitials (`.resultpage`) that simply recentre a fixed-measure card at every width — there is nowhere else to go from either screen. Rank reveal is a normal page inside the app shell (`.resultsview`, uncapped, single column) because tier 3 restores normal navigation.

The disclosure ladder is the thing to check across the three: finish shows only a score and an answered count; holding adds a finisher count but still no names or ranks; rank reveal is the first screen anywhere in the product allowed to show a rank, a neighbour's name and score, or `--rv-*` correctness colour.
