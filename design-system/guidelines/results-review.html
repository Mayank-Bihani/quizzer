Quizzer Design System · Artifact 4 of 5

Results, review & leaderboards

Three result screens exist because of a secrecy requirement, not a UX preference. The components below are grouped by what a student is allowed to know at that moment, and each group's forbidden information is stated in the component, not left to the implementer.

■
The disclosure ladder. 1 · finish-card (immediately after your last answer): own total score and count answered. Nothing else — no rank, no correctness, no distribution, no leaderboard, no "you beat N people". 2 · holding-state (until the hard deadline): own score, how many students have finished, countdown to unlock. 3 · after the deadline: rank, review with correct answers, distributions, boards. A component from tier 3 must never render while the window is open.
◇
Correctness colour lives only here. --rv-correct-*, --rv-wrong-*, --rv-skipped-* and --rv-notreached-* are review-only tokens. They must not appear in any Batch 3 component. Outcome is always carried by a glyph and a word as well as colour — ✓ Correct, ✕ Wrong, – Skipped, ◌ Not reached.

Pick sheet — Batch 4

ComponentABCRecommended

Open questions

  • Tie-break. Rendered as total time, ascending, shown in its own column. If ties instead share a rank (two 4ths, then 6th), every rank column and the reveal copy change.
  • Percentile / band language. Variant C of rank-reveal shows "Top 5%". With ~83 finishers a percentile is coarse and can identify individuals; flagged as speculative until you confirm you want bands at all.
  • Answer distribution basis. Assumed percentages are of students who answered that question, with n printed. Alternative is percentage of all participants, which makes skips visible — a different, arguably more honest chart.
  • Explanation availability. Assumed every question has an admin explanation. If some don't, the review card needs an explicit "No explanation provided" state rather than an empty panel.
  • Review window. Assumed review stays open indefinitely after the deadline. If it expires (to protect retired questions), History rows need an expiry state.
  • Overall board composition. Assumed "Overall" is the sum of section scores across the week. If it's an average or a separate quiz, the board switcher needs a definition line.
  • Marks scheme in the samples. Every printed number in this artifact uses +3 correct / −1 wrong / up to +0.5 bonus per question, so a 20-question quiz is out of 60 and the sample attempt totals 38.50. If the real scheme differs, only these sample values change — no layout does.
  • Speed bonus per question. Rendered per-question in review ("+0.3 bonus") and as one total in the breakdown. Confirm the bonus is computed per question, not per attempt.
  • Opting out of public names. The brief says real names are shown publicly. No opt-out is designed. If one is ever needed, `leaderboard-row` needs an anonymised state — say so now rather than later.
  • Medal emoji. Variant B of the leaderboard row uses 🥇🥈🥉, which contradicts the no-emoji content rule. If B wins, these ship as Lucide medal glyphs.
